# Studies and Findings
- Training is run to the indicated number of training steps (50k or 15k) across 256 parallel envs.
- The reported metrics of each study are on the model producing the highest success rate, not necessarily the final model. 
- Metrics are recorded in post-training Evaluation. The eval phase runs for 1024 episodes across 64 parallel envs.

## Reward Ablation Studies
- Purpose: To determine the impact of removing single rewards.
- Experimental setup:
  - Each study's reported values the average over 5 trials, each with a different RNG seed.
  - Each study was run to 50,000 steps.
- Findings (task success rate): 
  - Removing `approach-phase[absolute]` yielded the best: `0.8966796875`
  - Removing `grasp-phase[absolute]` yielded second best: `0.891015625`
  - Baseline: `0.87734375`
  - Success Rate is the proportion of episodes that terminate in success (i.e., lifting the cube to the height threshold).
  - Interestingly, a `Minimal` reward configuration in which the majority of rewards were disabled yielded only slightly degraded performance from baseline: `0.8685546875`
    - The Minimal config disabled the all of following rewards: `approach_distance`, `approach_alignment`, `aproach_phase[progressive]`, `approach_phase[absolute]`, `grasp-phase[progressive]`, `grasp_phase_terminal`,  `wrist_roll_pose`, `avoid_bumping_cube`, `safety_touch_table`, `time_penalty`
- Comments
  - The grasp phase is arithmetically "gated" by the approach phase. The grasp phase calculation is the product of the approach phase score and an additional grasp phase score. Thus, expectedly, the behavior of removing either of these rewards produces similar results.
- Observations:
  - Ablating the `absolute` approach phase reward yields a higher success rate than baseline.

## Vision Backbone Studies
- Three different vision backbones were evaluated. All were pretrained; none were updated or tuned during PPO backprop.
- Vision backbone variants:
  - The ResNet18: non domain specific to our task; used as a bootstrap
  - CNN1: Trained using synthetic data generated by playing back the policy model trained with the ResNet18 backbone.
  - CNN2: Trained using synthetic data generated by playback of the policy model trained on the CNN1 vision backbone.
- Performance:
  - The ResNet18 backbone performed worst. This expected: it was trained as a classifier on images out of domain.
  - Gen1 CNN: Significant leap in performance: ~5pp
  - Gen2 CNN: Minor improvement: ~0.5pp

## Individual Reward Contribution Studies
- Motivated the finding that the Minimal configuration (above) produced results similar to the baseline
- Rather than ablating rewards, we start with the minimal configuration and add in single rewards
- For the sake of time, reduced trials of this study to a single RNG seed per study (rather than 5) and reduced training time to 15k steps (rather than 50k)
- Observations:
  - Over the reduced 15k training budget, 8 of 13 configurations never achieve the success lift height. 
  - 2/13 achieve minimal success (`approach-distance` and `grasp-phase[progressive]`): ~1%.
  - 2/13 (`grasp-phase[absolute]`, `grasp-phase-terminal`) achieve success of 15-17%

## Composite Reward Contribution Studies
- Motivated by the Individual Reward Contribution studies, we once again start with a minimal reward configuration and add in rewards, this time combinations
- Observations
  - Adding `approach-phase-terminal`, `grasp-phase-terminal`  yields an impressive `0.833984375` success rate
  - Adding `grasp-phase[absolute]`, `approach-phase-terminal` yields an even better `0.859375` success rate
  - Adding all three (`grasp-phase[absolute]`, `approach-phase-terminal`, `grasp-phase-terminal`) yields a slightly degraded success rate of `0.7373046875`.
- Comments
  - The combination of the dense `grasp-phase[absolute]` reward (which as mentioned is the same as the `approach-phase`-based rewards except that it also include a grasp-specific factor in its product) combined with the sparse `approach-phase-terminal` reward is sufficient to push training to success even on 1/4 training steps budget.  
  - It's possible that the degraded performance of adding all three rewards can be attributed to random noise that would be adjusted for if we had run more trials and taken the mean
